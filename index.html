About & privacy

TeeDraw stores your players, draws and scores only on this phone. If anonymous usage stats are switched on, we count things like "a draw was made with 12 players in 3 groups" or "a round was finished at Monzi", plus a rough country/region worked out from the connection. Live events (optional): if you create or join an event and live sharing is set up, that event’s first names/nicknames, course handicaps, tee times and scores are stored online so people with the event link or QR can watch. Event codes are random (about 100 bits), never listed, and only people with the separate scorer link can change scores. Use first names or nicknames only – no surnames or phone numbers. Without live setup, events stay on this phone. We never send player names, phone numbers, scores per person or anything that identifies you, and we use no cookies and no tracking across other sites. This helps us show sponsors how many golfers use the app. Questions: contact the TeeDraw team.
